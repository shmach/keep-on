Keep on

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026

Your data never leaves your device
Overview

Keep on is a Chrome extension that helps you stay focused by detecting when you visit distracting websites during a timed session. It is built and maintained by Samuel Machado.

This extension does not collect, store remotely, or transmit any personal data. All session state and settings are stored exclusively on your device using Chrome's local storage API and are never sent anywhere.

What We Don't Do
No data collection
No remote servers
No analytics
No advertising
No account required
No third-party services
Permissions & Why We Need Them
tabs Detects when you switch to a tab on a blacklisted site so the grace-period timer can begin.
storage Saves your session state and settings (blacklist, grace period, sound preference) locally on your device only.
alarms Manages the session countdown timer reliably, even when the service worker sleeps.
notifications Shows a native browser notification when your focus session completes.
<all_urls> Required to inject the focus overlay into any page you visit, since blacklisted sites can be any domain. No page content is read or recorded.
Local Data Only

The extension stores the following data in chrome.storage.local on your device:

Session state — whether a session is active, when it started, its duration, and distraction counts. Settings — your blacklist, grace period, and alarm sound preference. This data is never transmitted and is cleared when you uninstall the extension.
